AGREEMENT
- Published TNT is a four-state swing/pre-swing timing algorithm, not a full-stride phase. It holds T=0 through stance and uses previous-cycle rate in early swing.
- Its transitions are not observed physical HS/TO. Current detector itself is geometric; downstream force dependencies exist throughout observations, FSM and reward.
- The active ankle and knee stance require further kinematic state/progression. Published validation uses a passive ankle and three able-bodied users.
- Exact paper thresholds cannot be directly reused in the inspected AB06/AB07 trajectories under the declared diagnostic angle convention.
- A no-GRF training contract requires removal of actor/critic force channels, force-gated state logic, force reward terms, and GRF-derived teacher/profile preprocessing under the strong reading.
- New feature/event contracts, adapted/retrained actor and critic, explicit startup, and independent validation are required.
